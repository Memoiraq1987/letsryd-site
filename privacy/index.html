Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy covers the letsryd.com website, run by RYD LLC in Seattle, Washington. When the RYD apps launch, they will have their own privacy policy.

What we collect

  • Your email address, if you join our waitlist.
  • An invite code we create for you, and the code of the friend who shared their link with you, if you came from one.
  • Which page you signed up on, and the date.
  • Standard technical logs, such as IP address and browser type, which the companies that host our website and server may keep for security.

If our server can't be reached when you sign up, the site saves your email and invite code in your own browser only, on your device.

How we use it

  • To send you RYD news and your launch invite or offer.
  • To count the friends you invite.
  • To keep the site secure.

We don't sell your information. This site has no advertising or analytics trackers.

Who we share it with

Only the companies that run the site for us: GitHub hosts this website and Render runs our server. The site's fonts load from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when a page loads. We may also share information if the law requires it.

Your choices

To stop our emails or have your email address deleted, write to hello@letsryd.com.

How long we keep it

Until you ask us to delete it, or until we no longer need it for RYD's launch updates.

Children

This site isn't meant for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page.
