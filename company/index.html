RYD RYD LLC · Seattle, Washington

A rideshare app built in Seattle, for Seattle.

RYD LLC is a Washington company building RYD, a rideshare app for the Seattle area.

What we're building

  • RYD for riders. Request a ride, see the price before you book, and follow your driver to the pickup.
  • RYD for drivers. Local drivers accept trips and see what they earn on every ride.

Where we are

RYD is preparing to launch in Seattle in 2027. We'll start offering rides once we hold the required Washington State, King County and City of Seattle licences.
